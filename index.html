What collectors ask

Quick answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Free, no account, no subscription — just your collection.

Do I need an account? Does it work offline?

No account needed. Your collection lives on your device and works with no signal in the store. Export/import backup is included.

What can I track?

Manga, manhwa and light novels — including your country's local editions. Create custom editions (like 2-in-1) with the title and volume count you need.

How is it different from MyAnimeList or AniList?

Those track what you watched. MangaShelf tracks what you physically own: edition, owned / missing / read separately, plus a grouped wishlist for hunting.

Is online sync coming?

Today backup is via export/import. Online sync for phone + tablet is in early testing — contact me from Settings if you want in.
